# Deploy Pipeline

:::steps{kind="secondary"}
. [icon="download" title="Install CLI"] Run `npm i -g xyd-js` first.
. [title="Authenticate"] Provide your **API token** to continue.
. [icon="hammer" title="Build"] Compile with `xyd build`.
. [icon="rocket" title="Deploy"] Ship the `.xyd/build/client` folder.
:::
